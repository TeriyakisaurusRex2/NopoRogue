Card Text Audit

Comparing each card's hand-written text against what the generator produces.

What you're seeing

Every card in the game has two sources for its description:

1. HAND-WRITTEN — the static effect: string we typed into cards.js. This is what's shipped today.

2. GENERATED — what generateCardText() produces from the card's effects:[] array (the actual mechanics). This is the future. Eventually, hand-written goes away.

Each card-face below renders BOTH so you can compare visually. The badge tells you whether they agree.

Badge meanings

DRIFT The two texts don't match. Either the hand-written has a typo / old style, OR the generator's not yet producing the right text. Inspect both, decide which is correct. MATCH Generated text equals hand-written. Safe to delete the effect: field on this card — generator is authoritative. NO HAND TEXT Card has effects:[] but no effect: string. Already migrated. Generator IS the source of truth. NO EFFECTS Card is hand-written only — no mechanics array. Generator can't help yet. Needs a new effect type, or it's a placeholder. ERROR Generator threw an exception. Bug — investigate the effects array or the renderer.

Why drift exists right now

We're mid-migration. Phase 1 (done) built the fmt primitives and centralised formatting rules. Phase 2 is what this audit is for — burn down the drift list card by card, either fixing the generator (if the hand-written form is what we want) or deleting the static string (if the generator's output is correct).

When a card shows DRIFT, look at the diff strip below the card faces — it shows the raw text side-by-side without HTML noise.
